# SCS Lab Website

**Steel & Composite Structures Laboratory (SCS Lab)**
Department of Architectural Engineering, Dong-Eui University, Busan, Republic of Korea.

Public website: https://kore03-creator.github.io/SCS/
GitHub repository: https://github.com/kore03-creator/SCS

## Pages
- `index.html` — Home, research areas and research methodology
- `professor.html` — Faculty profile, academic education and appointments
- `research.html` — Five focus areas and research process
- `publications.html` — Supplied bibliographic records: 16 international journal papers, 10 domestic journal papers, 15 conference presentations, and 6 patents. 26 journal DOI links.
- `members.html` — Laboratory director; undergraduate researchers 강경민 and 류연우 (third-year students); inquiry pathway
- `contact.html` — Engineering Building Room 625, address, email leejs@deu.ac.kr, and map search

## Technology
Plain static HTML, CSS and JavaScript, plus a locally hosted portrait and conceptual SVG illustrations.
GitHub Pages publishes the `main` branch root to https://kore03-creator.github.io/SCS/.

## Accuracy and privacy
- Faculty portrait is supplied by the director.
- Structural study SVG figures are illustrative; `assets/research-gallery.svg` contains the six research photographs and analysis figures provided for this website.
- Bibliographic records and DOI links were copied from the supplied publication list. Independent publisher-side DOI validation and typographic proofreading remain advisable.
- Official email and names/years of two undergraduate researchers were supplied and have been published. No student portraits or other personal details were provided.
- Recruitment wording invites consultation and does not guarantee admission, funding or an available position.
